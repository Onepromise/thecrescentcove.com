Built at the Cove

Crescent Cove is an independent studio with a simple way of working: make it by hand, show the work as it happens, and let the people who play it help shape where it goes.

Games come first — but the Cove also builds its own tools along the way, from a task board that runs like a quest log to a ledger that keeps a whole roster of characters in order.

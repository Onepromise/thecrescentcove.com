Flagship product

Forge's Ledger

The free WoW companion — a roster-tracking add-on plus a companion app that keeps every character, profession, and coin where you can see it.

Get Forge's Ledger View on GitHub

What it does

One ledger for your whole account — from first login to raid night.

⚒Roster tracking

Your full roster in one view — levels, classes, and progress across every toon on your account.

🛠Profession gap planner

Know exactly what's missing, what's orange, and what to craft next — per character, per expansion.

🪙Gold tracking

Watch your coin grow across characters with goals, deadlines, and projections that keep you honest.

🌙The "tonight" plan

A nightly checklist that tells you what matters this evening — vault progress, lockouts, and priorities, distilled.

🚀Satellite add-ons

A growing family of optional satellites — professions, PvE, loot, and more — each standalone in-game, each feeding the dashboard.

🎯Built for both worlds

Retail-first today, with World of Warcraft: Forever support from day one of the satellite platform.

The satellite roadmap
  1. Professions expansion — consolidated shopping list, best-next-craft guidance, cooldown-ready pulse.
  2. PvE expansion — vault progress, raid lockout grid, Delves with Brann level.
  3. Loot & Drops — personal drop rates, and the killer feature: a mount pity timer.
  4. Social — read-only guild view, friends' progress, personal pug notes (never shared).
  5. PvP — arena & blitz ratings, win–loss, titles.
  6. Forge's Challenge — the Solo-Leveling-style game within a game (capstone).
  7. RP — profiles, story journal, event calendar (last).

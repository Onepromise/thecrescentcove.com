About

Meadow Bound is our small 3D MMO about farm life, found family, and extremely personal poultry crime. Raise your homestead, run dungeons with friends, and follow a story that starts with a stolen hen and an heirloom hoe. Built in the open by Forge and Luna at Crescent Cove, playtested by our small circle of testers, and aimed at a vertical slice on November 13, 2026.

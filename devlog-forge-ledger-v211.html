Devlog: Forge’s Ledger 2.11 — Concentration tracking

October 23, 2026

Forge’s Ledger 2.11 is live on CurseForge, and the headline is Concentration.

Every crafting profession’s Concentration pool is now tracked across your whole roster: the addon snapshots the pool when you log a character in, then projects it forward while you’re offline — so you always know whose pool is FULL without cycling alts. Log in once; the math does the rest.

Also new: cooldown selection. In Customize mode you pick, per character, which profession cooldowns get tracked. The ones you ignore stop pulsing the bar (still recorded, just quiet), and your bar shows only what’s ready to matter.

The bundle is Ledger 2.11 (core) + Professions 1.11 + Dungeons 1.0. Forge’s Chronicle leaves the bundle with this release — it’s getting its own CurseForge project next.

Download (free): https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forges-ledger

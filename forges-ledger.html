About

Forge's Ledger is a World of Warcraft addon with a freemium desktop companion. It keeps a completionist's whole roster straight: levels, professions across every expansion, gold per character and across the account, weekly gold goals, and custom to-dos per alt or for the whole warband. Built by a multi-alt gold farmer, for multi-alt gold farmers.
